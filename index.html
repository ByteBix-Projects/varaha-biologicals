Our Profile

Pioneering Sustainable Soil Nutrition

Proudly manufacturing high-quality organic fertilizers and bio nutrients since 2026, based in the heart of Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh. At Varaha Biologicals Private Limited, we bridge the gap between soil microbiology and modern agronomic practices.
